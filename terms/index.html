Flirto Terms of Use and Community Guidelines

Version 2026-10-03.2

Community Guidelines

  • Be yourself: real name, real age, recent photos of you only.
  • Be kind. No hate, harassment, threats or bullying.
  • No nudity or sexual content in photos, and nothing sexual without clear consent.
  • Never ask for money, gifts, OTPs or bank details, and never send them.
  • No selling, advertising, links to other services or recruiting.
  • No one under 18, ever. Report anyone who might be.
  • Meet in public places first and tell a friend your plan (Safety help can share it).
  • Report anything that feels wrong. Reports are private.

Terms of Use

Who can use Flirto

You must be 18 or over and allowed to use a dating app where you live. One account per person, in your own name and with your own photos.

Your account

You sign in with Google or Apple. Keep that account safe; you are responsible for what is done with your Flirto account. You can turn on two-step sign-in in Me.

What you share

You keep the rights to your photos and words. You let us show them to other members inside Flirto so the app can work. Other members only see a rough distance, never where you are.

Safety and moderation

Photos and photo verification are checked by our team before others see them. You can block, unmatch or report anyone. We review reports within 24 hours and may warn, hide or ban accounts that break the Community Guidelines.

Flirto Gold, Boosts and Super Likes

Paid features are bought through Google Play or the App Store and follow their billing, renewal and refund rules. Cancel a subscription in your store account.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in Me → Delete my account. Your profile is hidden at once, and your photos, matches and messages are erased within 30 days. Information needed for an open safety review may be kept until it ends.

No ads, no selling data

Flirto shows no ads and does not sell your information.
